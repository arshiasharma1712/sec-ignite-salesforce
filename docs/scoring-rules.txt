Strategic Note to Teams:
The business rules and point values provided below are intended to serve as a baseline
reference. While you may use this exact matrix to build your prototype, teams are highly
encouraged to use their best judgment and business acumen to refine, adjust, or completely
redesign this scoring model. If you believe a different priority distribution makes better
business sense for a sales organization, implement it! Just ensure your routing engine still
functions flawlessly, and be prepared to defend your strategic decisions during your final
presentation.



Scoring Logic & Routing Business Rules
Your routing engine must calculate a total score for each lead based on the following
attributes.

Note: For testing purposes, expect the dataset to contain roughly 10% Critical, 20% High,
40% Medium, and 30% Low priority leads.


1. The VIP Override
   ●​ If a lead has VIP_Referral == True , immediately assign a score of 100 points, bypass
      the rest of the scoring matrix, and categorize them as CRITICAL. (Expect this tag to
      be rare).


2. Standard Scoring Matrix
For all non-VIP leads, calculate the score using the following criteria:

A. Company Size (Employee Count)

   ●​   Enterprise (1,000+ employees): +50 points
   ●​   Mid-Market (100–999 employees): +30 points
   ●​   SMB (1–99 employees): +0 points
   ●​   Fallback Rule: If the Company Size field is missing/blank, assign a default of +20
        points.

B. Recent Activity

   ●​   Requested Live Demo / Contact Sales: +35 points
   ●​   Attended Webinar: +25 points
   ●​   Downloaded Whitepaper : +15 points
   ●​   Visited Pricing Page: +5 points

   ●​ Fallback Rule: Any other recorded activity, unmapped action, or missing value receives
      +0 points.

C. Industry Vertical Multiplier (Bonus Priority)

   ●​ If the Lead is in the Technology sector: +15 points
   ●​ If the Lead is in the Healthcare or Finance sector: +10 points
   ●​ If the Lead is in the Manufacturing or Retail sector: +0 points


3. Priority Tiers
Once the total score is calculated, categorize the lead into one of the following tiers:

   ●​   CRITICAL: 85+ points (Must be prioritized at the top of the routing queue)
   ●​   HIGH: 60 – 84 points
   ●​   MEDIUM: 40 – 59 points
   ●​   LOW: 0 – 39 points


4. Seniority Routing Rule
   ●​ Leads categorized as CRITICAL (either by score or VIP status) require experienced
      handling.
   ●​ Your logic must attempt to route Critical leads to a Senior representative first.
   ●​ If all eligible Seniors in that region/vertical/language are at maximum capacity, fallback
      and route the lead to a Manager.
   ●​ Junior representatives cannot receive Critical leads under any circumstances.
