Judging Rubric (100 Total Points)

1. Core Logic & Routing Accuracy (25 Points)

   ●​ Does the engine successfully handle Data Quality by independently resolving messy
      strings, casing, and aliases?
   ●​ Are leads accurately scored and routed based on constraints like Region, Language,
      Vertical, and the Seniority hierarchy?
   ●​ Did the team implement a thoughtful, custom Tie-Breaker routing strategy (e.g.,
      scheduling algorithms) rather than just dumping leads on the first match?
   ●​ Bonus/Appreciation: Did the team go above and beyond by effectively integrating
      machine learning models to enhance their logic, and such additions will be highly
      appreciated by the judges.

2. Scale & Performance (20 Points)

   ●​ Does the strictly in-memory system successfully and rapidly process the 50,000+
      record scale dataset?
   ●​ Does the application remain stable and efficient without crashing or freezing during
      the heavy data spike?

3. Edge Cases & Resilience (15 Points)

   ●​ Did the team design a logical, defensible fallback strategy for missing fields rather
      than letting the system fail?
   ●​ Are overflow leads (when all eligible reps hit maximum capacity) correctly caught and
      routed to a visible manager review queue?
   ●​ Did the team provide automated tests that successfully run and prove their system
      handles anomalies gracefully?

4. Dashboard & UI Execution (20 Points)

   ●​ Does the dashboard demonstrate strong user empathy by presenting KPIs and queue
      features (like sorting or filtering) that a manager actually needs?
   ●​ Is the Rep Capacity effectively and visually tracked so workload limits are instantly
      clear?
   ●​ Do the core required action buttons function flawlessly, and did the team successfully
      implement any valuable additional controls or functionalities to enhance the
      dashboard?

5. Presentation & System Blueprint (20 Points)

●​ Did the team clearly articulate and defend their product decisions (fallback logic,
